EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW:
The following document outlines 10 high-impact, institutional-grade visual designs
and functional engines proposed for NewRa Atlas. These features build directly upon
the existing India Coverage Map philosophy—turning complex Indian electricity 
regulations, tariff schedules, Open Access economics, and grid codes into stunning,
interactive, and indispensable analytical tools for RE developers, corporate consumers,
and power traders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE FORMAT: FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[FEATURE 01] BHARAT INTER-STATE TRANSMISSION & LOSS TOPOLOGY VISUALIZER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Interactive Visual Mapping & Grid Economics
Inspiration: Enhancing the India Coverage Map into a multi-state wheeling router
Concept:
  - An interactive national transmission grid map showing Inter-State (ISTS) and
    Intra-State (InSTS) transmission corridors connecting RE surplus states 
    (Rajasthan, Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) to heavy load center states 
    (Maharashtra, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal).
Key Capabilities:
  - Origin-to-Destination Path Router: Click "Generator State" (e.g., Rajasthan) and 
    "Consumer State" (e.g., Maharashtra).
  - Automated Corridor Loss Gross-Up: Instantly calculates Northern Region (NR) ISTS 
    Loss + Western Region (WR) ISTS Loss + Maharashtra STU Transmission Loss + 
    MSEDCL Distribution Wheeling Loss.
  - ISTS Waiver Engine: Shows waiver eligibility (MoP 25-year 100% waiver rules vs 
    phased 25%/50% trajectory based on COD date).
  - Visual Design: Animated glowing energy particle lines traveling along state borders,
    hoverable substations (765 kV / 400 kV PGCIL nodes), and clean summary telemetry pill.


[FEATURE 02] 96-BLOCK TOD & DISPATCH ARBITRAGE DYNAMIC HEATMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Time-of-Day (TOD) Visual Intelligence & Scheduling
Inspiration: 15-minute Indian Grid Code (IEGC) Block Architecture
Concept:
  - A 24-hour, 96-block chromatic matrix comparing RE generation curves 
    (Solar bell curve / Wind evening peak) against state-specific TOD tariff bands.
Key Capabilities:
  - Visual 96-block chromatic heatmap with time on X-axis (00:00 to 24:00 in 15-min blocks) 
    and energy tariff delta on color spectrum (Deep Emerald for -20% rebate to Crimson 
    for +25% peak surcharge).
  - Commercial Arbitrage Calculator: Drag a typical 10 MW Solar generation profile 
    over the blocks to calculate:
      * Direct Consumption Value (Rs)
      * Night Rebate Opportunity (Shifting load into off-peak)
      * Banking Vulnerability (Peak-hour drawal restriction violations)
  - DSM Penalty Safe Corridor: Displays CERC/SERC Deviation Settlement Mechanism (DSM) 
    frequency bands (49.90 - 50.05 Hz) and financial penalty exposure.


[FEATURE 03] INTERACTIVE 26/51 CAPTIVE EQUITY & POWER-FLOW ARCHITECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Statutory Compliance & Corporate Structuring
Inspiration: Electricity Rules 2005 Rule 3 & Supreme Court Landmark Precedents
Concept:
  - A visual node canvas for modeling Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) equity holding 
    and proportional power consumption for Group Captive solar/wind parks.
Key Capabilities:
  - Interactive ownership slider: Generator (Lead Developer) equity vs Captive 
    Users (User A, User B, User C) equity.
  - Real-time Statutory Compliance Gauges:
      * Ownership Gauge: Checks "Total User Equity >= 26% with voting rights"
      * Consumption Gauge: Checks "Total Annual User Offtake >= 51% of generated power"
      * Proportionality Rule Gauge: Validates consumption matches equity within +/- 10%
  - Exemption Financial Value: Displays live savings from 100% exemption on CSS 
    (Cross-Subsidy Surcharge) and Additional Surcharge (AS), showing exact Rs Crores 
    saved vs risk of disqualification.


[FEATURE 04] MULTI-STATE REGULATORY RADAR & COMPARATOR ARENA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Comparative Intelligence & Market Attractiveness
Inspiration: Multi-dimensional radar benchmarking for RE deployment
Concept:
  - Compare up to 4 states simultaneously across 6 critical commercial dimensions 
    using an interactive SVG spider/radar chart.
Key Dimensions Evaluated (Scale 0-100):
  1. Base Grid Tariff Competitiveness (HT Industrial / Commercial rate)
  2. Open Access Surcharge Burden (Lower CSS + AS = Higher Score)
  3. Banking Framework Favorability (Annual vs Monthly cycle, 100% banking vs capped)
  4. Transmission & Wheeling Loss Efficiency (Lower technical loss = Higher Score)
  5. Green Energy Open Access (GEOA) Ground Implementation Ease
  6. Regulatory Stability & Order Timeliness (MYT predictability)
Interactive Output:
  - Automated "NewRa State Attractiveness Index" (e.g., Gujarat: 88/100, Rajasthan: 84/100, 
    Maharashtra: 72/100).
  - Side-by-side difference table highlighting the single most profitable state for a given C&I load.


[FEATURE 05] HISTORICAL TARIFF ORDER SCRUBBER ("REGULATORY TIME MACHINE")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Longitudinal Analytics & Rate Trend Modeling
Inspiration: Multi-Year Tariff (MYT) trajectory and inflation tracking
Concept:
  - An interactive horizontal scrubber timeline enabling analysts to travel 
    backward and forward across DISCOM tariff orders over the past 5 fiscal years 
    (FY22 through FY27 Drafts).
Key Capabilities:
  - Displays how energy charges, fixed demand charges, FAC/FPPAS, CSS, and wheeling 
    charges have evolved year-over-year.
  - Percentage CAGR calculation for electricity cost inflation in each DISCOM.
  - Predictive projection engine: Calculates expected tariff escalation for 15-year 
    PPA financial models based on historical true-up trajectory.


[FEATURE 06] 49 DISCOM VITALITY & TARIFF SHOCK RISK SCORECARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Utility Health, Financial Risk & Offtaker Security
Inspiration: Ministry of Power Integrated DISCOM Ratings
Concept:
  - A clean diagnostic scorecard for all 49 DISCOMs evaluating operational and 
    regulatory health factors that impact commercial PPA safety.
Metrics Tracked:
  - ACS-ARR Gap (Average Cost of Supply vs Average Revenue Realized, Rs/kWh).
  - AT&C Loss % vs SERC Mandated Target.
  - Regulatory Asset Overhang (Accumulated True-Up Deficit).
  - "Tariff Shock Risk Index" (High / Moderate / Low probability of imminent 15%+ tariff hike).
  - Visual status pill: Healthy (A+), Stable (B), Distressed (C-).


[FEATURE 07] INSTANT REGULATORY PASSPORT BUILDER WITH TAMPER-PROOF QR CODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Enterprise Workflows & Downstream Integration
Inspiration: GES/GEP Statutory Identity System for NewRa Lens & GridFlex
Concept:
  - A 3-step rapid wizard where users input:
      1. Consumer / Generator Coordinates (State, DISCOM, Connection Voltage)
      2. Contract Demand & RE Tech (Solar / Wind / Hybrid)
      3. Supply Mode (Captive, Group Captive, Third-Party, Green Open Access)
Output Generated:
  - Instant official "NewRa Regulatory Passport" containing:
      * Unique Passport URN (e.g., `GES-URN-MH-MSEDCL-2026-X99`)
      * Scannable verification QR Code linking directly to the live verified profile
      * Cryptographic SHA-256 data hash guaranteeing the tariff numbers match the SERC gazette
      * One-click PDF download & JSON webhook feed into NewRa Lens and GridFlex.


[FEATURE 08] GREEN ENERGY OPEN ACCESS (GEOA) RULEBOOK VS GROUND REALITY MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Policy Intelligence & Dispute Risk Reduction
Inspiration: Ministry of Power 100 kW GEOA Rules vs State SERC Divergence
Concept:
  - An interactive diagnostic matrix highlighting the exact policy conflicts between
    the Central Electricity Ministry (MoP) 100 kW mandate and individual state regulations.
Diagnostic Indicators:
  - Threshold Compliance: 100 kW notified vs 1 MW legacy restriction.
  - CSS Exemption Status: Whether green energy enjoys concession or faces full surcharge.
  - Banking Window: 30-day settlement vs monthly settlement.
  - Single-Window Portal Status: Fully automated portal vs manual physical application.
  - Actionable Advisory: "Litigation Risk Flag" with references to APTEL and High Court rulings.


[FEATURE 09] INTERACTIVE SANKEY ENERGY & LOSS WATERFALL ("THE BARREL VISUALIZER")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Energy Accounting & Physical Power Flow
Inspiration: Physical electron flow from generator busbar to consumer meter
Concept:
  - A flowing interactive Sankey diagram tracing 100 Units (kWh) generated at the solar farm:
      * -3.2 Units: CTU / STU Transmission Loss
      * -4.5 Units: DISCOM Wheeling Loss
      * = 92.3 Units Net Energy Delivered to Grid Node
      * -8.0 Units: In-Kind RE Banking Deduction (if routed to banking)
      * = 84.3 Units Net Energy Settled at Consumer Bus
Key Value:
  - Makes technical loss gross-up intuitive for corporate sustainability heads and financiers.
  - Hovering on any slice reveals the governing Tariff Clause and exact Rs loss value.


[FEATURE 10] NATURAL LANGUAGE CMD+K REGULATORY CALCULATOR BOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Category: Productivity, Search & Speed
Inspiration: Superhuman / Raycast style command palette intelligence
Concept:
  - Upgrades the existing Ctrl+K search dialog into a computational assistant.
Sample Queries Executable Directly in Search:
  - "MH 33kV landed cost with 3.20 PPA" -> Instantly shows Rs 5.12/kWh landed cost.
  - "Gujarat wheeling loss 11kV" -> Returns exact 6.00% + tariff order clause.
  - "Compare Rajasthan vs Karnataka solar banking" -> Opens side-by-side comparison.
  - "Is 150 kW eligible for Open Access in TN?" -> Returns GEOA status & minimum capacity rule.
